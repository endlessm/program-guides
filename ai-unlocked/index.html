AI Unlocked

How do I unlock what AI can do for me as I build the future I want?

Six quick stops, each one hands-on: play a round of snake, unlock a skill. You'll see what AI really is and learn the five Ds that keep you in charge of it: Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence and Data. About 30 minutes - the start of skills you'll carry into whatever comes next.

Exit ↗

AI (artificial intelligence) is technology that helps computers do things like recognize pictures, solve problems, make decisions and create new things.

Some AI learns from examples. That’s called machine learning: the more examples it learns from, the better it gets at a task.

Generative AI is a type of AI that creates things like stories, pictures, music and code. Gemini and Access AI are both generative AI.

Want to see how AI can learn? Teach an AI snake what to eat, then watch it play.

✓ Stop 1 unlocked

The first D is Delegation: deciding what you do, what AI does, and what you do together. AI is fast at making lots of options. You're the one who decides what's fun, what fits and what's yours.

Our rule for every piece: human first, human last. You start it, AI can help in the middle, and you decide what stays.

Build a snake game your way. For each piece, choose who makes it: you, AI, or the two of you together. Then play what you built. This one has walls, so unlike the first game you can crash and lose - just press play to go again.

✓ Stop 2 unlocked

The snake you taught learned a pattern from your examples - that’s machine learning. This AI works differently: it creates something new from your words. That’s generative AI, the kind you’ll use most - Gemini and Access AI both work this way.

The second D is Description: telling AI clearly what you want. AI can't read your mind. Every detail you leave out, it fills in with a guess.

Start with a short prompt and see what the AI makes. Then make it better with a clearer one.

✓ Stop 3 unlocked

The third D is Discernment: looking closely at what AI gives you and deciding if it's right, if it's good, and if it's what you asked for. AI can sound very sure and still be wrong.

✓ Stop 4 unlocked

The fourth D is Diligence: taking responsibility for what you make with AI. That means being honest about how you used it, keeping personal information private, and checking before you share.

Play Snake and eat the smart moves.

✓ Stop 5 unlocked

The fifth D is Data. Every conversation you have with AI leaves a record of how you worked: what you asked, how you steered, what you checked. You can learn from it, and so can the Coach in Access AI.

✓ Stop 6 unlocked

AI unlocked!

You know what AI is and how to use it well: decide who does what, describe it clearly, check what you get, own what you make, and learn from how you worked. You'll use all five in every session.

AI keeps changing fast - from machine learning, to the generative AI you used here, to agentic AI that can take actions for you, and bigger ideas still being built. The tools will keep getting more powerful. These five Ds are what keep you at the wheel, whatever comes next.
